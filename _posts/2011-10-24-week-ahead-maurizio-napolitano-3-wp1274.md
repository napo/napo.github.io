<p><strong>Last week</strong><br />
The past week has been an important week for open data in Italy.
I have been involved in first person in many of these events as an ambassador for OKFN.</p>

<p>Briefly summary</p>

<ul>
<li>The Italian government, in the minister of civil service and innovation, has created the portal <a href="http://dati.gov.it">dati.gov.it</a><br />I have been involved in the subsequent discussion, and I proposed &#8211; along with Lorenzo Benussi and Federico Morando &#8211; this wish list:<ul>
    <li>make a decree so all the P.A. data currently online are open data (the default license should change from closed data to open data)</li>
    <li>the italian data portal should be also in english</li>
    <li>have differents versions for the IODL &#8211; Italian Open Data License (so include also a version for the public domain and another for the attribution)</li>
    <li>an integration with the CKAN API</li>
        </ul></li>
<li>Among the initiatives launched by the Italian government also <a href="http://www.appsforitaly.org">appsforitaly</a> (I am involved in the organizing committee). The idea  born in May by many of the italian open data community, can finally begin.</li>
<li>ISTAT- the Italian national statistical institute &#8211; organized a seminar about open data with some talks and a discussion panel (where I was involved as Italy OKFN member).<br />The most interesting topics were:<ul>
    <li>The position of the EU  (some slide about the project LOD2)</li>
    <li>The current Italian situation seen as a starting point (very impressive the speech of dr. Miorondo &#8211; Regione Piemonte)</li>
    <li>The need for an infrastructure based on the data (geodata in particular)</li>
        <li>The interest on the growth of data journalism</li>
        </ul>
</li>
<li>Another initiative that I am involved, did this week is also <a href="http://www.wikitailia.it">Wikitalia</a>, whose first contribution was the opening data of Florence &#8211; <a href="http://dati.comune.firenze.it">http://dati.comune.firenze.it</a></li>
<li>This week I could not participate in OGDCamp in Warsaw but I thank Federico Morando because he presented my slides based on the tales of the entire Italian community &#8211; <a href="http://www.slideshare.net/Federico_Morando/ogdcamp-okfn-it">http://www.slideshare.net/Federico_Morando/ogdcamp-okfn-it</a></li>
</ul>
